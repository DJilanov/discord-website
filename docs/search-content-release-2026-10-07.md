# October 7 search and onboarding release

The community remains independent and unofficial, welcoming other guilds, both
factions and EU/NA players. KFC recruitment uses KFC's own website and invitation.
No membership, moderator-coverage or completed-event claims were invented.

## Implemented

- A join-oriented `/discord` title and description, retaining its self-canonical.
- Current channel reference, checked read-only through the bot on October 7:
  Start Here, introductions, useful-links, faq, scheduled-runs, tool-support and
  the current trading forums. No private channels or messages are published.
- One nine-class discovery section with direct Forever talent calculators;
  the existing onboarding guide explains useful questions and build evidence.
  No class doorway pages or unsupported best-build claims.
- Updated onboarding, group, guild-recruitment, friends and beta guides.
  Immediate listing publication, asynchronous Discord delivery and later
  moderation are distinguished from managed-event signup and website approval.
- Group times explain Central Europe, New York and dated UTC offsets. A dated
  walkthrough uses the owner's real future questing post, not fake activity.
- Rules, privacy and contribution instructions match the current flow. Sharing
  consent, private website reports and member-readable Discord reports remain
  distinct. Search indexing is not an endorsement.
- Exact pre-release guide revisions are retained for safe publication. Admin
  edits and drafts remain protected; the publisher uses optimistic concurrency.
