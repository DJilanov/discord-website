# Community Outreach Kit

Prepared September 30, 2026. Drafts for the owner to review and submit through
their own accounts. No external posts, direct messages, listing requests or
approvals are claimed by this document. Check the destination's current rules
and use only the relevant draft; do not broadcast every version everywhere.

> We're building a separate WoW Forever community for EU and NA players, both
> factions, and PvE, PvP and RP. You can keep your guild. This isn't a KFC
> recruitment server.
>

mandatory migration or replacement of the original guild's invitation. An

## Invitation to a Known Guild Organizer

> We're opening our Forever directory to other guilds as well as KFC. Would
> you be interested in listing yours? The useful details are your region,
> faction, usual hours, expectations and a contact you're happy to publish.
> No backlink or change of guild identity is required.
>
> https://www.wowforeverdiscord.online/guild-recruitment/new

Contact people with an existing relationship or through an explicitly open
submission route. A listing still needs the ordinary review; don't promise
priority placement or applicants. A refusal ends the request.

## Real Story Prompts

After an actual welcome session, ask the host: What happened? When? Which
question came up repeatedly? What should a newcomer know? What is the next
confirmed session? Ask participants before naming them or using screenshots.

For a guild-leader Q&A: Who fits your schedule? What is a normal night like?
How do trials and loot expectations work? What remains undecided? Keep the
organizer's answers distinct from the website editor's commentary. No invented
quotes, attendance or beta experience.
