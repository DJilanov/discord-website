# Discovery Before the Member Move

Research and decision date: September 30, 2026.

This is the next editorial and distribution plan, not a claim that the proposed
articles, listings or outreach have been published. It builds on the six live
guides and the September 30 homepage identity/KFC announcement release. The
owner is handling the member move separately.

## Decision

Publish selectively: two useful new resources, improve three existing guides,
and spend at least as much effort getting relevant people to discover them as
writing them. Do not create another batch of pages that all mean "join our
WoW Forever Discord."

The differentiator is practical help organizing a community, not being a larger
general-purpose game encyclopedia than Wowhead or Icy Veins. A visitor should
get a useful answer before being asked to join. There is no article-count,
backlink-count or keyword-frequency threshold that guarantees first place.

## Two New Resources

### 1. WoW Forever Beta Starter Checklist

- Proposed URL: `/guides/wow-forever-beta-starter-checklist`.
- Reader: someone with or seeking legitimate beta access who wants to get
  installed and find people to test with.
- Query hypotheses: `wow forever beta how to play`, `wow forever beta getting
  started`, `wow forever beta group`. These are editorial hypotheses, not
  measured search volumes or promised easy rankings.
- Different purpose from the Discord onboarding guide: game readiness and
  group preparation, not instructions for joining our server.
- Opening answer: concise access/install checklist, with the date the official
  instructions were checked and direct Blizzard links.
- Useful body: legitimate access routes; launcher version selection; what to
  do if the beta option is missing; where Blizzard handles technical support;
  what region, faction, availability and testing goals to include in a group
  request; checklist before a shared testing session.
- Original contribution: an organizer's practical preparation checklist and
  reusable group-request format, not a rewrite of Blizzard's announcement.
- Sources: Blizzard's beta instructions and current technical-support notices.
  Recheck immediately before publication. Do not turn week-one test details
  into claims about the current beta build.
- Visuals: an actual, permissioned launcher screenshot if available, or a
  relevant existing approved asset. Do not generate a fake launcher screenshot.
- Links: official access/support, existing group guide and `/discord`.
- Maintenance owner: named editor; review twice weekly during beta and after
  announced changes. When beta ends, update the same URL with a clear status and
  launch alternative; do not leave stale access promises.
- Publish gate: every game-specific claim has a primary source; no claim of
  hands-on testing without a real tester; no account linking, beta-code giveaway
  or purchase required by our community.

The beta is already live; this is preparation before the community migration,
not an article pretending the game has not entered testing. The official beta
page distinguishes access methods and installation steps. [Blizzard beta instructions](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24304160/the-world-of-warcraft-forever-beta-now-live)

### 2. WoW Forever Launch Checklist for Friends and Guilds

- Proposed URL: `/guides/wow-forever-launch-group-checklist`.
- Reader: a small friend group or guild officer agreeing how to start together.
- Query hypotheses: `wow forever play with friends`, `wow forever guild launch
  checklist`, `wow forever group preparation`.
- Different purpose from the group guide: a group's pre-launch decisions and
  contingency plan across several days, not advertising one dungeon or event.
- Deliverable: a readable checklist plus a blank, reusable Markdown planning
  template. No custom application or account required.
- Sections: region and usual hours; faction preferences; realm decision when
  official information exists; attendance expectations; joining late; voice
  preferences; shared contact point; backup plan; who confirms each decision.
- Label illustrative schedules as examples. Do not promise cross-region,
  cross-faction, realm-transfer or addon behavior without current primary
  confirmation. Keep unannounced realm details explicitly undecided.
- Use KFC organizers' prior raid coordination experience as attributed advice,
  not as evidence that Forever raids have already been run by this community.
- Links: the group/recruitment guides, current guild directory and `/discord`.
- Publish gate: an organizer reviews the template for practical use; a reader
  can use it without becoming a KFC member; no duplicated onboarding sections.
- Maintenance: review after new official realm/launch information and before
  release; update the resource rather than publishing date-keyword variants.

The current Blizzard announcement is the source of launch information, not
community speculation. This plan does not repeat exact regional launch times
that have not been independently verified. [Blizzard announcement](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24302093/carve-a-new-path-with-world-of-warcraft-forever)

## Google and AI Search

Google's guidance favors original utility and clear responsibility over mass
production or summaries that add little. Use a real author/editor, primary
sources, useful screenshots and honest update notes. There is no preferred
article length that makes this work. [Google people-first content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content)

For Google's AI features, keep the same indexable, accessible pages and
consistent visible text/structured data. No special AI schema or extra AI text
file is required. Google includes AI-feature traffic in Search Console's Web
report; do not assume a separate available report. [Google AI features guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features)

OpenAI distinguishes OAI-SearchBot from GPTBot: search eligibility and training
permissions are separate. Preserve public search access on the community site;
do not claim that enabling training improves recommendations. Check real
server logs and published crawler-network information if access is uncertain.
ChatGPT has no single universal first-place recommendation. [OpenAI crawler documentation](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots)

KFC's existing Bing/OpenAI restriction is a separate owner decision. If the
owner wants KFC itself discoverable there, review both robots.txt and Nginx,
retain private-route protection, and test the intended policy. Do not simply
disable security or copy that restriction to the community website.

## Measurements and Stop Rules

- Record the current baseline before publishing: Search Console query, page,
  country, device, impressions, clicks, CTR and average position. Treat a
  partially reported day separately; compare equivalent completed periods.
- Track `wow forever discord`, regional variants and the new resource intents
  as distinct groups. Do not infer search volume from a few sample results.
- Separate first-party entry sessions and Discord invitation clicks from
  actual joins and returning participants. An invitation click is not a join.
- Keep an outreach log with request date, rules checked, destination, submitted
  URL, response and accepted listing URL. Unanswered requests remain pending.
- Review AI mentions with fresh neutral prompts, recording product, date,
  locale, whether web search ran and exact cited URL. Do not seed the test with
  our website or count repeated prompted praise as earned discovery.
- If a page is not indexed, inspect the reason before writing another one.
  If it gets impressions but poor CTR, inspect query intent and position before
  repeatedly changing its title. If clicks do not become participation, improve
  the invitation/welcome experience before increasing content volume.
- Stop expanding a topic when the proposed article answers the same question
  as an existing guide, needs invented experience, or has no responsible owner
  to keep time-sensitive facts current.

## Not Planned

No bulk class/BiS/raid guides without tested Forever knowledge; no copied
Wowhead pages; no "best Discord" ranking that awards ourselves first place;
no keyword-stuffed region/faction permutations; no purchased links or fake
reviews; no manufactured search-and-click campaigns; no daily fake dates; no
domain move or another visual rebuild to chase an unproven ranking benefit.

The practical target is a small, credible set of resources people can use and
recommend now, ready to show real activity when the owner brings people over.
